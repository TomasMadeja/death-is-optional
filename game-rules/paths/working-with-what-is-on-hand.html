Path Mechanics

Material Armor

You body is enveloped with stone, metals and other materials, protecting you. When you gain material armor, add one body durability tracker of the same name. When gained, the ability will tell you it’s maximum size.

Gaining a second one overwrites the previous one. Once it reaches 0, you loose the material armor. You can drop the armor at will, automatically reducing it to 0.

The material armor is also consumed by your other abilities.

Starting Abilities

Armor Up

Action, cost(5 might)

Gain material armor (durability).

Pow: 1 material armor for each Success

Recovering durability

Material armor is only restored by using this ability. Repeated uses do not stack. The higher one remains.

Reshape

Action, cost(3 might)

Transform a (part of) solid object you touch into a rough structure, e.g. wall, pillar, or a bridge. You move the object/material a certain distance as part of tranformation.

Pow: +1m3 volume of material per Success.

Pow: Double volume of material every 4th Success.

Pow: Move material for +10m per Success.

Difficulty of materials

Not all materials are easy to, or meant to use. Sometimes, a check may be necessary to signify a heavy or fortified material. This should only matter in situations when the reshaping is significant, such as trying to break open into a fortress or trying to tear a material renowned for its strength.

Know what you are working with

You are skilled at identifying minerals, metals, and other solid materials.

Stich

Scenic Action, cost(4 might)

Repair tears small tears in structures, mend cracks, or solder together metals. You require half as materials as by normal means, or may take half the time doing so.

Hit: 1st Success - 1 tick.

Hit: 1 tick per Success.

States of matter

Detailed shapes

Free Action, Last Action, cost(3 might), conditional

When using Reshape, instead of manipulating the object into rough shape, you only change its exterior. You can create detailed changes to the surface of the object.

The dice cost of this ability is added to the Reshape roll.

Liquid stone

Free Action, Last Action, cost(3 might), conditional

When using Reshape, while transforming or moving, the material behaves as if it was liquid. It can be funneled through thin gaps, and reformed on the other side. It can allow targets to sink into the transforming material, and become trapped as it hardens, or vice versa.

The dice cost of this ability is added to the Reshape roll.

Dhokra

Create Mold

Action, cost(5 might)

Touch a target and envelop it (or part of it) with a mold made of material you can touch. The mold hardens and takes on all the details of what it contains.

The mold can contain 1m3 large object, or a part of it.

Hit: +1 durability clock size per Success.

Pow: + 5m3 volume per Success.

Pow: double the volume every 3rd Success

Using the mold for crafting

The mold you created can be reused for crafting copies by either you or other craftsman. It has to be filled with a liquid material capable of hardening, such as molten steel. The material is allowed to harden, and the mold broken.

When using your abilities, you can fill the mold using your abilities with other materials, and split it without destroying the mold.

Using the mold in combat

You can use the mold to restrain enemies, and protect allies. While the target is wrapped in the mold, they also gain benefits from it’s durability.

An enemy fully wrapped in the mold may have difficult time moving, but they are also protected from attacks. Wrapping part of their body will hinder them less, but it will also not gain them protection.

You can use this to your advantage, as a way of blocking powerful attacks on yourself or an ally.

Maintanance

Tools of the trade

You gain a skill in tools used to craft or repair structures and solid objects.

File away

Action, cost(5 might)

Touch an object, and instantly remove large layer of its surface in form of filing. This can remove rust and other impurities, just as well as create a poisonous cloud if the material was toxic.

You can affect an area of 1m2. The dust raises around 1 meter of the surface.

Pow: +1m2 area per Success.

Pow double the area every 4th Success.

Whakairo

Carve

Action, cost(5 might)

Touch an object and remove parts of the object as if by carving it out. You may carve both exterior and interior, and eject the removed material as long as the design allows it. You can create complex designs and mechanically functional structures and tools.

You can affect 1m3 large (part of) object.

Hit: +2 ticks of damage per Success.

Hit: +2 ticks of damage every 3rd Success.

Pow: +1m3 per Success

Pow: double the volume every 4th Success

Regarding construction

When using Carve to create a tool, it might might not be on par with a crafted piece. It does not provide benefits of actions such as tempering, that might affect durability or flexibility of the object.

The detail is only significant based on what you are trying to create.

Broadening the Horizons

Mechanics at play

Free Action, Final action, cost(2 drive)

Ask 1 questions about inner workings of a mechanism. This can be either a physical mechanism, such as clock, or abstract mechanism, such as how an ability works.

The answer will be truthful, based on what you could have possible observed, but might not be complete if the question is too general.

Pow: +1 question every 2nd Success.

Inkwork

With touch and access to material, you can create fine mineral and metal filings as an ink, and any living or nonliving surface as a canvas. You can create detailed and colorful imagery.

Creating a piece could be considered a Scenic Action and would be akin in pace to current day tattooing.

Mokurito

Draw without the matrix

Action, cost(5 might)

Touch a target, and imprint on them a large splatter of fine metal or stone filing. The mokurito can form a pattern of your choosing.

The drawing remains until scrubbed off.

You can affect 1m3 large (part of) target.

Pow: +1m3 per Success.

Pow: double the volume every 4th Success.

...the fine print

Final Action, cost(5 might)

Touch a living creature affected by your mokurito. Choose one of the following effects:

  • push them 5m in any direction
  • allow them to hold in the air as if levitating
  • ease or hinder them by 1

Hit: (push) +5m movement per Success

Hit: (push) double the movement every 6th Success

Hit: (ease/hinder) +1 ease/hinder every 3rd Success

Sandpainting

Sandcastles

Action, cost(5 might, 1 soul)

Touch an object, and shape it into countless miniature particles. You can shape it in 3D space into a complex scene.

The scene can form walls, clouds and complex details. You determine how dense they are, and their architecture.

The appearance is akin to a sand based hologram. It can look lifelike enough to fool someone a hundred meters away. If materials allow it, you can use colors.

The design can be animated, moving on predetermined paths. You can decide on complex behaviors, such as mimicking animals, and even people.

The area scene can occupy area of 100m3, and has damage clock size of 3+level/2.

Pow: +100m3 size per Success.

Pow: +1 damage clock size per Success.

Damaging the scene

Acts to damage the scene require you to roll to defend it. Taking wounds affects it as other objects, by ticking its damage clock.

If someone is actively destroying solid structures within the scene, you can choose to let them fall apart as a Free Action. Doing so reduces the damage taken by 1 tick.
