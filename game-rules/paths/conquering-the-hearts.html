Path mechanics

Court

You have access to a group of non-player characters who form your court. Abilities allow you to interact with these court members and draw power from them.

Court size (how may characters are in your court) starts at 2, and increases every 2 levels (up to 7).

Adding court members requires the non-player character to meet following condition: the character is willing to be member of the court. This does not require any in-character confirmation, only willingness confirmed by the GM.

Replacing court members can only happen during a free scene. Court members are not meant to be constantly replaced for sake of mechanical advantage. Such situation can be handled by non-player unwillingness to join the court.

Starting Abilities

Reading the room

You are skilled in determining the opinion of others about you.

Inspire

You are skilled in inspiring and motivating others.

Unyielding

Action, cost(1 might)

Reduce a setback of a clock in a scene related to: inspiration, motivation, call to action.

Hit: 1st Success - 1 tick

Hit: 1 tick every 2nd Success.

Hit: 6th success - if setback is reduced to 0, gain 1 tick instead.

Survey the battlefields

Free Action, cost(4 might)

Learn what motivates your target by observation, study or interacting with them. Ask 2 questions about the target that tell you something about their: motivation, inspirations, drive.

Pow: +1 question every 2nd Success.

Advise me!

Free Action, Final Action, cost(3 might)

Ask member of your court for advice in form of 1 question. Regardless of distance or situation, you receive their response within limits of their knowledge.

Pow: +1 question every 2nd Success.

Does the ability communicate with the target?

Depending on how you choose to flavor the ability, you are either communicating via supernatural means, natural means, or image what the advisor would say. Depending on the form of communication, it might not require speaking.

If you do choose to directly communicate by supernatural means, this ability on its own does not allow you to hold arbitrary conversations. It only allows to ask for advice.

Leadership

Inspiring leadership

Free Action, cost(2+ drive)

Target you can communicate with regains 1 drive die for every 2 drive dice spent.

Adoration of the crowd

Free Action, cost(Unusual)

Willing target who can communicate with you spends 2+ drive dice. You regain 1 drive die for every 2 drive dice spent.

Kingcraft

Lead by example

Free Action, cost(3 might)

When you and the target are working together this round, their roll is eased.

Pow: +1 target per Success.

Inspiring Speech

Scenic Action, cost(1 soul)

Perform a speech and motivate audience members (including yourself) to achieve a goal. Formulate a short goal. When taking actions to achieve the goal, the audience members can consume points from the inspiration pool. 1 point eases the roll by 1. The benefit ends once the pool is empty, or the goal is achieved, failed, or given up on.

Inspiration pool is a point pool with size of 3.

Pow: +1 inspiration pool size per Success.

Pow: +2 inspiration pool size every 4th success

Courtly tricks

Impressive repertoire

Free Action, Final Action, cost(1 soul, 1 drive), Conditional

When making a roll a member of your court is skilled in, you also roll as if you had the same skill. Dice cost of this ability is added to the pool for that roll.

Dreams of the conquered

Dreams of my subjects

Free Action, Final Action, cost(1 soul)

Gain extra drive from one of your court members, on top of your usual drives. Lasts until end of the scene or until you use this ability again.

Social Aptitude

Outnumber but not outgunned

Social Scenes: Increase overwhelm by 2∗ your level for soul rolls.

Knock for things

You have a superhuman instinct for understanding the social side of people. You can easily figure out where a specific groups of people tend to meet up, their social norms, and overall social quirks. This includes catching on quick to their forms of entertainment.

Building an army

You are skilled in recruiting, hiring, and related haggling for price.

Kingly Presence

Rouse the masses

Scenic Action, cost(1 soul)

Social scene: Your speech motivates the masses. Must be related to: motivation, inspiration. You can affect a small crowd up to 50 people.

Hit: +2 ticks per Success.

Hit: +1 tick every 3rd Success.

Pow: Double the crowd per Success.

Kingly roar

Free Action, cost(6 might, 2 drive), conditional

Social scenes. When making a roll as part of making a statement, you increase the overwhelm number of you ability by 2.

Pow: +1 overwhelm per Success.

Treasures

Lend me a hand

Free Action, Final Action, cost(1 soul, 2 drive)

Gain access to 1 ability belonging to a member of your court to use as your own. You keep it until the end of the scene, or until you use this ability again.

Are there any restriction?

To use an ability you must know about it, and the court member must be willing to lend it. You can not use an ability that is intentionally kept secret or restricted. There are no other requirements.
